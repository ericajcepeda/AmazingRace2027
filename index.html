Riverbat Bat Lab · Night Shift

The Amazing Race:
AI Bat Edition

Tonight, R.B. the Riverbat was supposed to fly to the next stop on the race. But an overnight update scrambled ECHO, the Bat Lab's navigation assistant, and ripped the flight plan into five Flight Fragments. Each fragment is locked inside a different system. Fix them all, collect the fragments in order, and the Riverbat will know where to land.

How the race works

  • ✍️ Write every fragment in your Mission Log. You'll need them all, in order, at the end.
  • 🚶 After each level, a Station Card somewhere in this room holds the Launch Code for the next one. Go find it!
  • ⬆️ You can scroll back up any time to see what you've already done.
  • 📱 Watch for texts and emails from R.B. and ACC leadership. They may have hints! Tap 📬 at the top to re-read them.
  • 💜 Our values keep the Riverbat flying: courage, compassion, joy, and yes.
